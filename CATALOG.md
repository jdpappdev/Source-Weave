# Skill catalog

| Skill | Purpose | Companion skills (optional) | Maturity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [`research-workflow`](.agents/skills/research-workflow/SKILL.md) | Plan and conduct web, repository, video, and supplied-material research; synthesize evidence and route next steps. | `youtube-digest`, `transcript-to-experiment`, `apply-tutorial` | Initial formulation; statically reviewed, not behaviorally benchmarked. |

Companion skills are optional integrations. This repository neither includes nor claims ownership of them. The research workflow should still provide a useful result when a companion skill is unavailable, while stating the resulting limitation.

Optional artifact formats: [research brief](.agents/skills/research-workflow/references/research-brief-template.md) and [source log](.agents/skills/research-workflow/references/source-log-template.md).
